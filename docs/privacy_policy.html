Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Your privacy matters. Swarika is an offline music player designed to let you enjoy music stored on your device. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app accesses and how it is handled.

1. Information We Collect

Swarika does not collect, store, or share personal information. The app does not require user accounts, registration, or login.

Swarika does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking services to monitor your activity.

2. Permissions

Swarika requests media or audio access permission on Android so it can find and display music files stored on your device.

This permission is used exclusively to:

  • Find audio files available on your device.
  • Display your local music library.
  • Play the selected audio files.

Swarika does not upload, transmit, or share your music files with external servers. You can revoke this permission at any time through your device settings. However, revoking it will prevent the app from accessing your local music library.

3. Data Storage and Sharing

Swarika operates locally on your device. It does not transmit personal information, music files, or usage information to external servers.

Your music files remain under your control on your device.

4. Third-Party Services

Swarika does not integrate advertising, analytics, tracking, or other external services that collect personal information.

5. Children's Privacy

Swarika does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users. The app does not provide account creation or personal data submission features.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the app's features or privacy practices change. Any updated version will be published on this page, along with its revised update date.

7. Contact

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, you can contact the developer through the Swarika GitHub repository .
